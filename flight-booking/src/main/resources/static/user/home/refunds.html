Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand that travel plans can change. Our cancellation and refund guidance is here to help you understand your options before you travel.

Refund guidance

Flexible bookings

Flexible fares may allow changes or cancellations with reduced fees, depending on fare conditions.

Non-refundable bookings

Some bookings may not be eligible for a cash refund, although taxes or selected charges may still be recoverable.

Processing times

Where eligible, refunds may take several working days to appear depending on the original payment method.

Things to keep in mind

  • Refund eligibility can depend on fare type and booking conditions.
  • Changes made close to departure may be subject to additional restrictions.
  • Some services or add-ons may not be refundable once confirmed.

Need support?

If your plans have changed and you need help reviewing cancellation options, you can manage your booking or contact support for further guidance.

Typical reasons for requesting changes

Customers commonly request cancellations due to scheduling changes, travel restrictions, personal emergencies, or updated travel plans.
